# Create a meeting

## Before you start

Select the correct chapter in the admin web app. You need permission to create meetings. If you cannot access **Meetings** or create a calendar entry, ask your chapter administrator to check your role.

## Steps

1. Select **Meetings** in the sidebar. A monthly calendar appears.
2. Select the meeting date. The **Create Meeting** form opens.
3. Fill in **Start Date & Time**, **End Date & Time**, the timezone and **Title**. Complete the description and invitee options needed for this meeting.
4. Choose **External meeting link** and paste your meeting URL, or choose **TransparencyHub meeting** for an in-app meeting.
5. Select **Submit** once. Check that the meeting appears on the calendar. For an in-app meeting, wait for its generated link before copying or sharing it.

## If it does not work

Check the required fields and any message shown by the form. Do not repeatedly submit while it is processing. If the meeting is still missing, tell Nia which step failed and include a screenshot without passwords or sign-in codes.

Recording is a separate task: [Record an external meeting](/platform/record-external-meeting).
