# Associations

Choose a chapter after sign-in, then open **Association** to see its association and chapter hierarchy. The page displays an organization chart and chapter search. An administrator with the appropriate chapter permission can open the association or a chapter, and create a chapter or subchapter from the hierarchy.

The admin area is scoped to the selected chapter. Use the chapter selector to switch between chapters where your account has a role. **Settings** manages configuration for the selected chapter; it is not an association creation wizard or an association deletion screen.
