# Sign up and view your association

## Sign up a new association

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the website form">
    Go to the [Transparency Hub Network association sign-up form](https://transparencyhubnetwork.ai/#signup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the association details">
    Provide the association or organization name, your name, email address, optional phone number, number of chapters, and number of members. You can also upload a logo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the request">
    Select **Get Started — It's Free**. The website confirms that the team will contact you about setting up the trial.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for admin access">
    The admin web app does not create the association account. Sign in after the team provides access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View the association hierarchy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a chapter">
    Sign in and choose a chapter where you have an admin role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Association">
    Select **Association** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the hierarchy">
    Use the organization chart or search to find the association, a chapter, or a subchapter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a record">
    Select the association or a chapter to view its available details and actions.
  </Step>
</Steps>
