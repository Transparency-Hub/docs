# Configure membership types

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Membership Type settings">
    Select **Settings**, then open the **Membership Type** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or edit a type">
    Start a new membership type, or use an existing row's edit action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete General Information">
    Enter the name and description. Configure the duration or fixed end date, renewal and deactivation behavior, privacy, and application form template. If you enable **Automatic Recurring Payments**, choose the reminder frequency shown by the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Price Configuration">
    Add each due with its amount and frequency. Configure its chapter account mapping and tax options where required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Workflow Settings">
    Choose whether applications require approval, whether application fees are paid separately, whether approval happens after payment, and whether renewals require approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Notification Settings">
    Enable the supported application, expiration, and renewal email stages. Open a stage's template control when you need to edit its message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the membership type">
    Submit the final step, then confirm that the type appears in the Membership Type table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The recurring payment controls do not display or guarantee a specific retry schedule.
</Note>
