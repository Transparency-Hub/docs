# Edit or cancel a meeting

## Before you start

Select the chapter that owns the meeting. You need permission to change that meeting.

## Edit a meeting

1. Open **Meetings** and find the calendar date.
2. Select the meeting, then use its edit action.
3. Change the relevant details in **Update Meeting** and select **Submit**.
4. Reopen the calendar entry and check the saved details, including the timezone and meeting link.

## Cancel a meeting

1. Open the meeting from **Meetings**.
2. Choose the cancellation action available for your role.
3. Read the confirmation before confirming cancellation.
4. Return to the calendar and check the result. Do not assume notifications were sent unless the app confirms them.

If an action is missing, ask your chapter administrator to check your meeting permissions. If a change fails, tell support the meeting title, chapter and what appeared on screen.
