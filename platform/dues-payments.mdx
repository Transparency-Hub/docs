# Dues and transactions

Configure dues when creating or editing a membership type under **Settings → Membership Type → Price Configuration**. The step provides due amount and frequency, account mapping, and tax options.

The **General** settings tab displays chapter accounts and payment connection flows including **Setup Stripe Account** and **Connect GoCardless**. GoCardless mandates are also displayed there. The app's payment choices can vary by chapter and checkout context; see [Payment setup](/integrations/payment-gateways) for the admin controls actually shown.

Open **Transactions** to see the chapter's transaction table with **All** and **Recent** views. **Upload Transactions** opens the upload flow; **Generate Statement** opens the statement flow. The **Finance** dashboard is a separate page and requires finance viewing permission.
