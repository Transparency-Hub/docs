# Manage dues and transactions

## Add dues to a membership type

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the membership type">
    Go to **Settings → Membership Type**, then create or edit a type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Price Configuration">
    Continue to the **Price Configuration** step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a due">
    Enter the due amount and frequency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map the account and tax">
    Choose the applicable chapter account and configure tax options when required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the membership type">
    Continue through **Workflow Settings** and **Notification Settings**, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View and upload transactions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Transactions">
    Select **Transactions** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a table view">
    Use **All** or **Recent** to change the records displayed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start an upload">
    Select **Upload Transactions** and follow the upload form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the processed upload">
    Return to the transaction table and upload archive to confirm the imported records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Generate a statement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Transactions">
    Select **Transactions** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the statement form">
    Select **Generate Statement**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the statement options">
    Choose the chapter account, start date, end date, and report type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Submit the form and use the generated report provided by the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>
