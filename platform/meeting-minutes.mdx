# Meeting minutes: choose a task

Choose the type of minutes you are working with:

- [Upload minutes manually](/platform/upload-minutes)
- [Review generated minutes](/platform/review-generated-minutes)

Uploading a file and generating minutes from a recording are different tasks. Uploading minutes does not start a meeting recording. To prepare a recording, see [Record an external meeting](/platform/record-external-meeting).
