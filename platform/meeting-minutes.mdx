# Minutes

Open **Minutes** in the admin sidebar. The page shows an **Uploads** table and an **Upload Minutes** action for roles with create permission. Entries can be opened and managed according to the selected chapter's minutes permissions.

For an external meeting, connect Google Meet, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams in **Settings → Meeting Integrations**, supply its meeting link when scheduling, and enable **Record and Generate Minute**. The recording bot joins to record and transcribe; generated minutes can be reviewed in the app after processing. Processing time and content can vary, so check generated text before sharing.

For the in-app TransparencyHub meeting option, meeting controls and recording are handled in the meeting experience. Consult the specific meeting details for available recording and minutes actions.

The admin **Minutes** page also supports manual uploads. It does not promise an **Export → PDF** transcript selector or unrestricted access to all association members.
