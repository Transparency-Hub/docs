# Manage meeting minutes

## Upload minutes manually

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Minutes">
    Select **Minutes** in the admin sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the upload">
    Select **Upload Minutes**. This button requires minutes create permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the form">
    Add the minute details and file requested by the upload form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the upload">
    Save the record, then return to the **Uploads** table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review or edit the entry">
    Open the uploaded record and use the actions permitted by your chapter role.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Generate minutes from an external meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the meeting provider">
    Go to **Settings → Meeting Integrations** and connect Google Meet, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the meeting">
    Open **Meetings**, choose a date, and enter an external meeting link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable recording">
    Turn on **Record and Generate Minute** and select the connected provider that matches the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the meeting">
    At the scheduled time, the visible recording bot joins to record and transcribe the meeting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the result">
    After processing, open the related minutes in the app and check the generated text before sharing it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For a generated TransparencyHub meeting, use the recording controls available inside its meeting experience.
