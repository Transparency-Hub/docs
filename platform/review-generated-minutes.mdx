# Review generated minutes

## Before you start

The meeting must have been recorded successfully. For an external provider meeting, **Record and Generate Minute** must have been enabled with a matching connected provider. See [Record an external meeting](/platform/record-external-meeting).

## Steps

1. Wait until the meeting recording and processing have finished. There is no guaranteed completion time.
2. Open **Minutes** in the admin app and locate the entry for the relevant meeting and chapter.
3. Open the generated minutes and review the text, names and decisions before sharing it.
4. Use the editing or sharing actions available for your chapter role.

## If minutes are missing

Check that you are in the correct chapter and that recording actually started. If processing failed or no result appears, tell support the chapter, meeting title, meeting time and provider. Do not create another recording or upload just to retry generation unless support advises it.

For minutes you already have as a file, use [Upload minutes manually](/platform/upload-minutes).
