# Record an external meeting

## Before you start

You need meeting-management permission for the chapter. In **Settings → Meeting Integrations**, connect the provider used by the external meeting link. See [Connect a meeting provider](/integrations/meeting-providers) if none is connected.

## Steps

1. Open **Meetings** and select a date to create a meeting, or open an existing meeting and choose its edit action.
2. Choose **External meeting link** and enter the Zoom, Microsoft Teams or Google Meet URL.
3. Turn on **Record and Generate Minute**.
4. Select the connected provider that matches the link. Correct any provider/link mismatch shown by the form.
5. Select **Submit**. Reopen the meeting to check its saved details.
6. At the scheduled time, look for the visible recording bot in the external meeting. Recording and processing must complete before generated minutes are available.

## If the bot does not join

Check the chapter's provider connection, meeting URL and saved recording option. Provider admission or access settings can prevent the bot from entering. Ask support to investigate if these checks do not resolve it; do not share provider passwords or tokens.

This guide covers external provider meetings. A **TransparencyHub meeting** uses the recording controls inside its own meeting experience.
