# Select and manage chapters

## Switch chapters

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the chapter selector">
    Use the chapter selection control in the admin app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search if needed">
    Enter a chapter name when the list is long.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the chapter">
    Choose a chapter where you have an active role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the selection">
    Check the chapter name and logo at the top of the sidebar. Members, transactions, meetings, and settings now use this chapter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create a chapter or subchapter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Association">
    Select **Association** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where to add it">
    In the organization hierarchy, open the association to create a top-level chapter or open a chapter to create a subchapter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Create Chapter">
    Start the chapter form from the available hierarchy action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the chapter details">
    Complete the required chapter fields and review the privacy and configuration options shown in the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Submit the form, then find the new chapter in the organization hierarchy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Creating or editing chapters requires the corresponding chapter permission.
</Info>
