# Chapters

The admin sidebar displays the selected chapter's name and logo. Choose another chapter through the chapter selection flow when your account has access to more than one.

Open **Association** to view the chapter hierarchy, search it, and open a chapter's details. Permitted admins can create chapters or subchapters from this screen. Chapter permissions govern whether you can view or change the hierarchy.

Members, membership types, transactions, meetings, and settings are loaded for the selected chapter. Switch chapters before working on a different chapter's data.
