# Manage members

## Add one member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the chapter">
    Confirm the chapter name at the top of the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Members">
    Select **Members** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the member form">
    Select **Add Member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the application">
    Enter the required member information and choose the applicable membership type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the member">
    Submit the form. The new record appears in the chapter's member list or application workflow according to its state.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View or edit a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the member">
    Open **Members**, then use the search and membership type filter to narrow the table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the row menu">
    Use the menu at the end of the member's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an action">
    Select **View** to read the member's application, **Edit** to change permitted details, or **Send Activation Email** when activation is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save any changes">
    Submit the edit form and confirm the member table reflects the update.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Upload members from CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Upload Members">
    From the Members submenu or Members page, select **Upload Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a membership type">
    Select the membership type for the upload. The upload actions remain unavailable until a type is selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the template">
    Select **Download Upload Template** and enter the member data in the supplied format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the CSV">
    Start **Upload Member CSV**, choose the completed file, and submit it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for processing">
    The upload runs in the background. The app notifies you when processing finishes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the upload archive">
    Use the archive table on the upload page to inspect the upload and any reported results.
  </Step>
</Steps>
