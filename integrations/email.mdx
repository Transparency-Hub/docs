# Configure email notifications

## Update chapter notification controls

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the chapter">
    Confirm the chapter at the top of the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open General settings">
    Go to **Settings → General**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find member notifications">
    Locate the member notification settings section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make and save changes">
    Update the available controls and save them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configure membership lifecycle emails

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a membership type">
    Go to **Settings → Membership Type**, then create or edit a type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set recurring reminders">
    In **General Information**, configure the reminder frequency when **Automatic Recurring Payments** is enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Notification Settings">
    Continue through the membership type flow to **Notification Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose email stages">
    Enable the supported application, expiration, and renewal email stages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit a template">
    Open the template control for a supported stage and update its content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Submit the membership type flow to retain the changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To read notifications sent to your admin account, open **Profile → Notifications**. Opening an unread item marks it as read.
