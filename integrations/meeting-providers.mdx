# Meeting providers

Open **Settings → Meeting Integrations** in the selected chapter. The page has provider cards for **Zoom**, **Microsoft Teams**, and **Google Meet**. Select **Connect** to complete the provider's authorization flow. A connected card shows the account email and connection date. **Disconnect** opens a confirmation dialog; existing events are not changed, while new external recordings require a connection.

Once a provider is connected, the page also offers a default recording preference for new events. To record a meeting, create or edit it under **Meetings**, use an external meeting link, turn on **Record and Generate Minute**, and select a connected provider compatible with that link. The form warns if the chosen provider does not match the link. A visible bot joins to record and transcribe.

A **TransparencyHub meeting** is an in-app meeting location; its link is generated by the app and its recording controls are in the meeting experience. The external provider connection is for bot recording of external links.

If you wish to remove the Zoom app from your Zoom account as well, manage it in [Zoom Installed Apps](https://marketplace.zoom.us/user/installed). The admin card's **Disconnect** action removes the chapter connection.
