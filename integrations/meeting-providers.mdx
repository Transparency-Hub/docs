# Connect a meeting provider

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the chapter">
    Confirm the chapter whose meetings you want to record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Meeting Integrations">
    Go to **Settings → Meeting Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a provider">
    Find **Zoom**, **Microsoft Teams**, or **Google Meet**, then select **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize access">
    Complete the provider's authorization flow and return to the admin app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Check that the provider card shows the connected account email and connection date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the recording default">
    If desired, enable **Enable recording by default for new events**. This control becomes available after at least one provider is connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use the connection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an external meeting">
    Open **Meetings**, choose a date, and paste an external meeting link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable generated minutes">
    Turn on **Record and Generate Minute**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the matching provider">
    Choose the provider that matches the link. The form warns when the provider and link do not match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the meeting">
    Submit the form. A visible bot joins the external meeting at the scheduled time to record and transcribe it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disconnect a provider

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the provider card">
    Return to **Settings → Meeting Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Disconnect">
    Use **Disconnect** on the connected provider and confirm the dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review existing meetings">
    Existing meetings remain unchanged, but you cannot create new recordings with that provider until you reconnect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove the Zoom app from the Zoom account as well, use [Zoom Installed Apps](https://marketplace.zoom.us/user/installed).
