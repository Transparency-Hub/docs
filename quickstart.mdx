# Set up your chapter

Before you begin, make sure your association account has been created and your administrator has assigned you a chapter role. New associations must first use the [website sign-up form](https://transparencyhubnetwork.ai/#signup).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and select your chapter">
    Sign in to the admin web app. In the chapter selector, choose the chapter you want to manage. Check the chapter name at the top of the sidebar before changing any data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the dashboard">
    Open **Dashboard**. Review chapter health, chapter admins, members, geographic distribution, and contributions. Use the date range and cadence controls to change the workflow chart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review general settings">
    Open **Settings → General**. Review chapter accounts, payment connections, directory fields, birthday broadcasts, the chapter join link, member notifications, and finance visibility.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a membership type">
    Open **Settings → Membership Type** and start a new type. Complete **General Information**, **Price Configuration**, **Workflow Settings**, and **Notification Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the application form">
    Open **Settings → Application Forms**. Create or edit the form template that applicants should complete, then associate it with the relevant membership type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members">
    Open **Members** and select **Add Member**, or use **Upload Members** for a CSV import.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review membership workflows">
    Open **Workflow → Application** to review new applications. Use **Workflow → Renewal** for upcoming and in-progress renewals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect meeting providers">
    If you plan to record external meetings, open **Settings → Meeting Integrations** and connect Google Meet, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule a meeting">
    Open **Meetings**, choose a date on the calendar, and complete the meeting form. Select either an external link or a generated TransparencyHub meeting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize files and projects">
    Use **Drive** to store chapter files. Use **Projects** to create a project board, assign tasks, and track progress.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Each page checks your role permissions. Ask a chapter administrator to update your role if a required action is unavailable.
</Info>
