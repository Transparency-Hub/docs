# Use Fundraising

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Fundraising">
    Select **Fundraising** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a campaign">
    Select **Create Campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the campaign">
    Enter the campaign information requested by the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Submit the form and confirm that the campaign appears in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit later">
    Open the campaign row menu and select **Edit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
