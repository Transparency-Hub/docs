# Use the dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the chapter">
    Choose the chapter you want to review, then open **Dashboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the reporting period">
    Use the date range control on **Chapter Health Status**. Choose **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, or **Yearly** to change the chart grouping.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a workflow stage">
    Select a bar in the workflow chart to open the corresponding application records for that period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review chapter information">
    Check the **Chapter Admins** card, the members table, the world map, and the contributions chart. Each section displays only when your role has the required permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a detailed dashboard">
    Expand **Dashboard** in the sidebar and choose **Finance**, **Fundraising**, **Membership**, or **Meetings** for a focused view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use **Members** for the full member table, **Transactions** for financial records, and **Workflow → Application** for the full application queue.
