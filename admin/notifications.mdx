# Read notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the chapter">
    Confirm the chapter you want to work in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Notifications">
    In the sidebar, open **Profile → Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a notification">
    Select a notification row to read its full content. Opening an unread item marks it as read.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move through the list">
    Use the pagination controls to view older notifications.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change notification settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open General settings">
    Go to **Settings → General** for the chapter's member notification controls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a membership type">
    Go to **Settings → Membership Type** and create or edit a type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure its email stages">
    In **Notification Settings**, enable and edit the supported application, expiration, and renewal email stages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the membership type">
    Submit the membership type flow to keep the notification changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Profile → Notifications** is your inbox. It is not a global email template editor.
</Note>
