# Use Contacts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Contacts">
    Select **Contacts** in the sidebar. The list contains people who attempted self check-in but were not on the member list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the list">
    Move through the contact pages and inspect the available details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export contacts">
    Select **Export CSV** to download the full contacts list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a contact">
    Use the delete action on a contact and confirm the dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>
