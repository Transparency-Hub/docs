# Process applications and renewals

## Review an application

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the application workflow">
    In the sidebar, open **Workflow → Application**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a stage">
    Select **Awaiting Payment**, **Awaiting Approval**, **Completed**, or **Declined**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a record">
    Select an applicant to view the submitted application form and the actions available at that stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the available action">
    Approve, decline, or move the application forward as allowed by your workflow permissions and its current state.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review a renewal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the renewal workflow">
    In the sidebar, open **Workflow → Renewal**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a stage">
    Select **Upcoming Renewal**, **Awaiting Payment**, **Awaiting Approval**, **Completed**, or **Declined**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the member record">
    Review the renewal information and complete the actions available for its current stage.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change the workflow rules

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the membership type">
    Go to **Settings → Membership Type** and create or edit the applicable type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Workflow Settings">
    Move to the **Workflow Settings** step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the required rules">
    Configure application approval, separate application fee payment, approval after payment, and renewal approval as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue and save">
    Continue to **Notification Settings**, review the email stages, and save the membership type.
  </Step>
</Steps>
