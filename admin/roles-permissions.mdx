# Roles and permissions

Open **Settings → Roles** to view roles for the selected chapter. If your role permits it, create a custom role or edit a role's permissions and save. Default role names cannot be edited. The app warns about unsaved changes when switching roles or navigating away.

The permission table covers areas such as chapters, members, finances, meetings, minutes, workflow stages, general and membership settings, roles, and activity logs. Each area has **view**, **create**, **edit**, and **delete** controls where applicable. The admin sidebar and pages check these permissions; a role without access may see a disabled action or an access denied page.

Assigning an existing role to a member is handled in the member management flow. The available roles and permissions come from the selected chapter; the app does not present a fixed plan based Owner/Admin/Moderator matrix.
