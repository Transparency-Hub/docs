# Manage roles and permissions

## Create a role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Roles">
    In the sidebar, open **Settings → Roles**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new role">
    Select the option to create a role and enter its name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose permissions">
    For each available area, enable the required **View**, **Create**, **Edit**, and **Delete** permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the role">
    Review the settings and save. Default role names cannot be renamed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit an existing role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the role">
    Open **Settings → Roles** and choose a role from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change its permissions">
    Update the permission switches needed for that role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save before leaving">
    Save the role. If you switch roles or leave with unsaved changes, the app asks whether to discard them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assign a role to an executive

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Executives">
    In the sidebar, open **Settings → Executives**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the executive">
    Locate the person whose role you want to change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the role">
    Use the row's edit action, select an existing chapter role, and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  You cannot edit or unassign a Super Admin through the standard executive actions.
</Info>
