---
title: "List event attendees / RSVPs"
openapi: "GET /v1/events/{event_id}/attendees"
description: "Who is invited to an event, their RSVP (`rsvp_status`, `did_rsvp`) and whether they attended."
---

Who is invited to an event, their RSVP (`rsvp_status`, `did_rsvp`) and whether they attended. `owner` marks the organiser.

**Required scope:** `events:read`
