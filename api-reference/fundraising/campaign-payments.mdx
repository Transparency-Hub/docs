---
title: "List campaign payments"
openapi: "GET /v1/fundraising/campaigns/{campaign_id}/payments"
description: "The donations made to a campaign."
---

The donations made to a campaign — who gave, how much, and whether the money has arrived.

`payment_status` is `completed` once the payment gateway has confirmed it; only completed donations count towards the campaign's `total_raised`. `member_name` is filled in when the donation is linked to a member. For the full payment record, follow `transaction_id` to `GET /v1/transactions/{transaction_id}`.

**Required scope:** `fundraising:read`
