---
title: "List campaign payments"
openapi: "GET /v1/fundraising/campaigns/{campaign_id}/payments"
description: "Who gave to a campaign."
---

Who gave to a campaign. Amounts live on the linked transaction (`GET /v1/transactions/{transaction_id}`).

**Required scope:** `fundraising:read`
