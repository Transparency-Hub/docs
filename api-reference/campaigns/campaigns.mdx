---
title: "List communication campaigns"
openapi: "GET /v1/campaigns"
description: "Communication campaigns (email / SMS / WhatsApp / push) with schedule and delivery `stats`."
---

Communication campaigns (email / SMS / WhatsApp / push) with schedule and delivery `stats`. Templates, audience segments and credit costs are not exposed. Filter by `status`.

**Required scope:** `campaigns:read`
