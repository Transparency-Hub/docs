---
title: "List Transactions"
openapi: "GET /v1/transactions"
description: "Retrieve the chapter payments, newest first."
---

Retrieve the chapter payments, newest first. Filter by `member_id`, `status` (exact match on the gateway status, e.g. `success`) and `from` / `to` on the payment date.

**Required scope:** `payments:read`
