# Core concepts

An **association** groups its chapters. The admin app operates in a **selected chapter**: the chapter name appears in the sidebar, and chapter roles determine which screens and actions are available. **Association** displays the hierarchy and lets permitted admins manage chapters and subchapters.

A **membership type** belongs to chapter settings. Its configuration includes general information, dues, application and renewal approval rules, and notification settings. **Members** shows chapter members; **Workflow → Application** and **Workflow → Renewal** show their records by stage.

**Meetings** are scheduled in a calendar or list. A meeting can use an external link or a generated TransparencyHub in-app meeting link. External recording uses a connected Google Meet, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams account. **Gatherings** is a separate admin screen for events and tickets.

**Transactions** lists chapter financial records and offers transaction uploads and statements. **Minutes** is a separate screen for viewing and uploading minutes, with actions governed by the chapter's minutes permissions.
