# Before you begin

## Check the selected chapter

Every admin task runs in the selected chapter.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look at the sidebar">
    Confirm that the chapter name and logo at the top of the sidebar match the chapter you intend to manage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change chapters when needed">
    Open the chapter selector and choose another chapter where your account has a role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the context again">
    Before adding members, changing settings, or uploading transactions, recheck the selected chapter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understand permissions

Your chapter role controls which pages and actions you can use. Permissions cover viewing, creating, editing, and deleting records in areas such as members, finances, meetings, workflows, minutes, settings, roles, and activity logs.

## Know where records live

| Task | Admin area |
| --- | --- |
| View association and chapter hierarchy | **Association** |
| Manage people | **Members** |
| Configure membership categories and dues | **Settings → Membership Type** |
| Review applications and renewals | **Workflow** |
| View and upload financial records | **Transactions** |
| Schedule online meetings | **Meetings** |
| Review or upload minutes | **Minutes** |
| Store and share chapter files | **Drive** |
| Plan work on boards and timelines | **Projects** |
| Read your notifications | **Profile → Notifications** |

<Note>
  **Meetings** schedules meetings. **Gatherings** is a separate area for events and tickets.
</Note>
