# Use the Transparency Hub Network admin app

This guide follows the admin web app released from `Staticwebsite-App` `origin/main` at commit `926ab69`, checked September 16, 2026. Screens and actions are scoped to the chapter you select and the permissions assigned to your role.

## Start here

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up your association">
    If your association does not have an account, complete the [association sign-up form](https://transparencyhubnetwork.ai/#signup) on the Transparency Hub Network website. The team will follow up to set up your account and trial.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Once your access is ready, sign in to the admin web app with your administrator account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a chapter">
    Choose a chapter where you have an admin role. The chapter name and logo appear at the top of the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a task">
    Follow one of the task guides below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Set up a chapter" icon="gear" href="/quickstart">Complete the initial admin setup in a practical order.</Card>
  <Card title="Manage members" icon="users" href="/platform/members">Add, edit, or upload chapter members.</Card>
  <Card title="Set up membership types" icon="list" href="/platform/membership-types">Configure dues, workflows, and notifications.</Card>
  <Card title="Schedule a meeting" icon="calendar" href="/platform/events">Create in-app or external meetings.</Card>
  <Card title="Drive and projects" icon="folder" href="/platform/drive-projects">Organize files and track chapter work.</Card>
  <Card title="Other admin tools" icon="wrench" href="/admin/other-tools">Use fundraising, tasks, gatherings, elections, and logs.</Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  If a page or button is missing, your chapter role may not have permission to use it.
</Info>
